Your first lap

Start in the sim.
Grow from there.

You do not need a drone to begin. A radio and a simulator are the fastest, least expensive way to learn the sticks.

01

Try a simulator

Simulator practice is key to learning the essentials—and crashes do not destroy drones. All you need is a computer capable of playing video games and a radio controller (RC).
We recommend RadioMaster radios equipped with ELRS ↗.

Come Spectate or Fly

We race quadcopters (aka quads) with 31 mm, 3-inch, 5-inch, and 7-inch propellers, using analog or HDZero video systems.

We host practice meetups and official races at the club field, plus special events throughout the Vancouver Lower Mainland.

Fly legally in Canada

Know before you arm.

Microdrones under 250 g have their own rules. Small drones from 250 g to 25 kg generally require registration and the appropriate pilot certificate. Where and how you fly determines whether an operation is Basic, Advanced, Complex, or Special.

Rules change and advertised events can require special permission. Always confirm your operation with Transport Canada; this overview is not legal advice.

About KwadsRUs

Grassroots racing,
built by pilots.

KwadsRUs Racing Club brings FPV pilots together in Metro Vancouver to race, build skills and share the hobby. What began as a group of enthusiasts meeting to fly has grown into an active MultiGP chapter with organized events and a welcoming local community.

Our outdoor club field gives pilots room to set technical courses, practise safely and spend a full day racing. Exact event and field access details are shared with each race listing.
