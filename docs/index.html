Your first lap

Start in the sim.
Grow from there.

You do not need a quad (short for quadcopter) to begin. A radio controller and a simulator is all you need to learn to fly!

01

Try a simulator

Simulator practice is key to learning the essentials—and crashes do not destroy quads. All you need is a computer capable of playing video games and a radio controller (RC).
We recommend RadioMaster radios equipped with ELRS ↗︎ .

Build or Buy Your Quad

Start with a radio, then goggles, batteries, charger and quad(s). These Canadian FPV shops can help with pre-built quads, compatible gear and replacement parts.
Ask us before buying : we can help you choose compatible gear for the classes you want to race in.

Come Spectate or Fly

We race quads in different classes: 31 mm, 3-inch, 5-inch, and 7-inch propellers, using analog or HDZero video systems.

We host practice meetups and official races at the club field located in Pitt Meadows, as well as special events throughout the Vancouver Lower Mainland.

Fly legally in Canada

Know before you arm.

Micro drones under 250g have their own rules. Small drones from 250 g to 25 kg generally require registration and the appropriate pilot certificate. Where and how you fly determines whether an operation is Basic, Advanced or Special.

Rules change and advertised events can require special permission. Always confirm your operation with Transport Canada; this overview is not legal advice.

About KwadsRUs

Grassroots racing,
built by pilots.

KwadsRUs Racing Club brings FPV pilots together in Metro Vancouver to race, build skills and share the hobby. What began as a group of enthusiasts meeting to fly has grown into an active MultiGP chapter with organized events and a welcoming local community.

Our outdoor club field gives pilots room to set technical courses, practise safely and spend a full day racing. Exact event and field access details are shared with each race listing.
